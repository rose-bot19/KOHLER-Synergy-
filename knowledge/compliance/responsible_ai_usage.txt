KOHLER RESPONSIBLE AI USAGE GUIDELINES

AI systems used for company work should operate within approved
business purposes.

Employees should not enter confidential or sensitive company
information into an unapproved AI service.

AI-generated content should be reviewed when it is used for important
business, legal, financial or customer-facing decisions.

Automated actions affecting external parties should use appropriate
approval and monitoring controls.

AI systems should provide evidence or source information when making
company-specific factual claims where practical.
