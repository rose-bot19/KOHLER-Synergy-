KOHLER BUSINESS TRAVEL REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Employees travelling for approved business purposes may claim reasonable
travel expenses according to their employee grade.

Hotel limits:
Grade 1-4: ₹8,000 per night
Grade 5-7: ₹12,000 per night

International travel requires manager approval before booking.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of returning.

Original receipts or approved digital receipts should be retained for
reimbursement.

Personal expenses are not reimbursable unless explicitly approved.
