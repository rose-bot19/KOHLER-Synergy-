KOHLER WARRANTY POLICY

Smart toilets are covered by a limited warranty.

Smart Toilet Warranty:
The standard warranty period for eligible Kohler smart toilets is 3 years from the original purchase date.

Warranty coverage may apply to:
- Manufacturing defects
- Electronic control failures
- Sensor failures

Warranty does not normally cover:
- Accidental damage
- Improper installation
- Unauthorized modifications
- Damage caused by misuse

Customers may be asked to provide:
- Product model number
- Serial number
- Proof of purchase

For warranty questions requiring model-specific verification, customer support should request the model number and serial number.
