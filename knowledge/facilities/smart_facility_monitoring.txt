KOHLER SMART FACILITY MONITORING GUIDELINES

Connected commercial facilities may monitor:
- water flow
- flush counts
- occupancy
- continuous leaks
- sensor diagnostic status

A continuous leak should trigger investigation because prolonged water
loss can result in unnecessary consumption and potential property damage.

High-traffic facilities may require increased cleaning and maintenance
frequency based on usage data.

Maintenance dispatches should include location, detected condition,
severity and relevant sensor information.
