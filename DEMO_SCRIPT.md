# KOHLER Synergy - 1 to 3 minute demo script

**0:00-0:20 - Problem**

"Enterprise teams have information spread across policies, product documentation, support systems and workflows. A normal chatbot can answer questions, but it cannot reliably move from understanding to action. KOHLER Synergy is designed as an adaptive enterprise AI layer."

**0:20-0:55 - Support workflow**

Enter:

> My name is Rose. I have a KOHLER Veil smart toilet and it is leaking. What should I do?

Show the grounded support response and the Agent Activity panel.

**0:55-1:15 - Warranty**

Ask:

> Is it under warranty?

Then explain that Synergy can retrieve company evidence instead of guessing.

**1:15-1:35 - Action**

Ask:

> Create a support ticket.

Show the generated ticket ID.

Then ask:

> What was my ticket number again?

Show conversational continuity.

**1:35-1:50 - Persistence**

Reload the page and show that the conversation is still present. Click Save Chat and show it in Saved Conversations.

**1:50-2:15 - Governance**

Ask for a high-risk action such as sending an email. Show that Synergy pauses and asks for approval before execution.

**2:15-2:35 - Close**

"The prototype brings together retrieval, tools, memory, persistence, auditability and human-in-the-loop governance. The same connector interfaces can later be backed by production systems such as ServiceNow, Salesforce, Gmail or Microsoft 365."
